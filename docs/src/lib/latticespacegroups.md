# LatticeSpaceGroups

Lattice geometry, and the space groups it induces. See [Lattices](@ref) for building them and
[Symmetries](@ref) for the site permutations they induce.

This package is re-exported by the umbrella, and also usable on its own — it depends on
StaticArrays and nothing else.
